About

AI × Chemistry

Doctoral researcher at the Pollice Research Group, University of Groningen, working at the intersection of machine learning and chemistry. My research focuses on AI-driven catalyst design and data-driven molecular discovery — combining computational workflows with ML to accelerate chemical space exploration and enable inverse molecular design.

Previously completed a Master's in Chemistry at Nanjing University in the group of Prof. Xie Jin, specialising in organometallic chemistry, photocatalysis, and DFT reaction mechanism studies.

Research Focus

🤖

AI-Driven Catalyst Design

ML workflows to predict catalyst activity and selectivity — rational design without exhaustive screening.

🔭

Chemical Space Exploration

Data-driven navigation of vast molecular spaces to surface promising synthesis candidates.

🔄

Inverse Molecular Design

Generative AI to design molecules and catalysts with target properties from the ground up.

⚛️

ML + Quantum Chemistry

Merging machine learning with DFT for accurate molecular property prediction at scale.

📊

Reaction Discovery

Mining chemical literature to build predictive models for novel chemical transformations.

🏭

Data-Driven Catalysis

Predicting regioselectivity and key outcomes in homogeneous catalysis from data.
